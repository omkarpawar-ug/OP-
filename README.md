# 🌊 calm-cli

> Keep yourself calm and clear, right from your terminal.

Developers spend hours staring at complex logs, failing tests, and cluttered terminals. **calm-cli** is a zero-dependency Python script that clears your screen and guides you through a quick, centering breathing exercise to reset your focus.

## ✨ Features

- **Zero Dependencies:** Built entirely with Python's standard library. No `pip install` required.
- **Visual Reset:** Automatically clears your cluttered terminal to reduce visual noise.
- **Guided Pacing:** Uses the 4-4-6 breathing technique (Inhale 4s, Hold 4s, Exhale 6s) to physically lower heart rate and stress.
- **Centering Wisdom:** Displays a randomized, stoic quote before every session.
